## 📖 Application Overview

The **GPS Sales & Inventory Management System** is an enterprise mobile web application and Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for store operations across **GPS MUSCAT**, **Gulf Pioneer Star**, and **Gulf Pioneer Smart** business locations.

The platform enables authorized staff to record point-of-sale transactions, check live inventory balances, process incoming stock shipments, audit inventory adjustments, and print digital receipts across physical branches—even when network connectivity is temporarily unavailable.

## 📲 End-User Installation Guide (Google Chrome Only)

> [!CAUTION]
> **GOOGLE CHROME REQUIRED**  
> This application is optimized for **Google Chrome**. Using Google Chrome ensures that Progressive Web App (PWA) installation, offline receipt storage, and automatic background data synchronization function reliably.

### Browser Requirement

- **Supported Browser:** **Google Chrome** (Desktop PC or Android Mobile/Tablet).
- **Supported Devices:** Windows 10/11 Desktop/Laptop PC, Apple Mac, Android Smartphones, and Tablets.

---

### Method 1: Desktop App Installation (Windows / Mac)

Install the application directly on your computer so it opens in its own window like a desktop program:

1. Launch **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. Open the official web application URL provided verbally or via internal channels by your IT administrator / management.
3. In the Chrome address bar, click the **Install App** icon 🖥️ (on the right side of the address bar).  
   _(Or click Chrome's **3 dots (⋮)** menu $\rightarrow$ **Save and share** $\rightarrow$ **Install GPS Sales & Inventory...**)_
4. Click **Install** to confirm.
5. The app will launch in its own standalone window and create a **GPS Sales & Inventory** shortcut on your Desktop.

---

### Method 2: Mobile / Tablet Installation (Android)

For staff operating on store floor mobile devices:

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your smartphone or tablet.
2. Navigate to your official store web application link.
3. Tap the **3-dot menu icon (⋮)** in the top-right corner of Google Chrome.
4. Select **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
5. Tap **Add** when prompted.
6. The **GPS** app icon will appear on your smartphone home screen and app launcher. Tap it anytime for a full-screen experience.

---

### Method 3: Pinning to Taskbar / Home Screen

For quick 1-click access during daily shifts:

- **Windows PC:** Right-click the **GPS Sales & Inventory** icon on your Desktop $\rightarrow$ Select **Pin to Taskbar**.
- **Android Mobile:** Touch and hold the **GPS** app icon on your home screen $\rightarrow$ Drag it to your primary dock bar.

---

### First-Time Permissions

1. Open the **GPS Sales & Inventory** application shortcut.
2. When prompted by Google Chrome, allow storage permissions so sales transactions can be saved locally on your device during offline periods.

## 📱 User Guide

### 1. Logging Into the System

1. Launch the **GPS Sales & Inventory** app.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.
3. Select your authorized corporate email account provided by management.
4. Upon authentication, you will be directed to the main operational interface.

---

### 2. Selecting Active Branch

1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the top-right corner of the application header.
2. Under **Active Branch**, choose your current physical store location (e.g., **Al Khoud Branch** or **Ghala Branch**).
3. The catalog, stock quantities, and sales logs will automatically update to reflect your active branch location.

---

### 3. Recording a Sale (`Add Sale`)

1. Tap **Add Sale** (🛒) on the bottom navigation bar.
2. **Search Product:** Type the product name or SKU into the search field and select the item from the suggestions.
3. **Quantity & Pricing:** Specify the item quantity. The total price is calculated automatically.
4. **Payment Method:** Select **Cash**, **Card**, or **Bank Transfer**.
5. **Customer Information:** (Optional) Enter customer contact details or order notes.
6. Tap **Complete Sale Transaction** to record the sale.

---

### 4. Viewing Sales History (`Sales`)

1. Tap **Sales** on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Review total revenue, transaction counts, and recent sales cards.
3. **Date Filters:** Filter transactions by _Today_, _Yesterday_, _This Week_, _This Month_, or _Custom Date Range_.
4. **Digital Receipt:** Tap any transaction card to view, print, or review the itemized receipt.

---

### 5. Receiving Stock Inward (`Add Stock`)

1. Tap **Add Stock** on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Select the incoming product from the stock catalog.
3. Enter **Received Quantity**, **Unit Cost**, **Supplier**, and **Batch Number**.
4. Click **Receive Stock Inward** to update the branch inventory balance.

---

### 6. Stock Adjustments (`Amend Stock`)

1. Tap **Amend Stock** on the navigation bar.
2. Select the product requiring adjustment.
3. Enter the **New Physical Quantity**.
4. Select the adjustment reason (_Damaged Goods_, _Expired Stock_, _Recount Discrepancy_, or _Branch Transfer_).
5. Add audit notes and click **Submit Audit Adjustment**.

---

### 7. Stock Level Monitoring (`Inventory`)

1. Tap **Inventory** on the navigation bar.
2. Search and browse the product catalog for your active branch.
3. View real-time stock balances, prices, and low-stock warnings.

---

### 8. Working Offline & Data Sync

- **Offline Mode:** If internet connection drops, you can continue recording sales and stock updates uninterrupted.
- **Auto Sync:** Once internet connection is re-established, pending transactions automatically sync with the cloud database.
- **Manual Sync:** Tap **Sync Now** inside the Profile screen at any time to trigger a manual data refresh.

---
